Supplier C — Service and support schedule
Demonstration document. No actual supplier offer.

1. sla / availabilityPercent
Monthly service availability commitment: 99.99%, excluding scheduled maintenance of up to four hours monthly.

2. sla / responseMinutes
Critical incident response within 10 minutes.

3. sla / resolutionHours
Target critical incident resolution: 2 hours; this is a target rather than a guarantee.

4. sla / serviceCredits
15% of monthly fees for each 0.1 percentage point shortfall, capped at 50%; repeated failures permit termination without penalty.

5. support / supportHours
24x7 telephone, portal and dedicated engineer support for all severities.

6. support / supportChannels
Support channels include telephone, email and portal.

7. support / supportCommitments
An escalation manager is assigned to critical incidents.
