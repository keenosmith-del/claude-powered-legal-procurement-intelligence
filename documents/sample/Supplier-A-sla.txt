Supplier A — Service and support schedule
Demonstration document. No actual supplier offer.

1. sla / availabilityPercent
Monthly service availability commitment: 99.5%, excluding scheduled maintenance of up to four hours monthly.

2. sla / responseMinutes
Critical incident response within 60 minutes.

3. sla / resolutionHours
Target critical incident resolution: 12 hours; this is a target rather than a guarantee.

4. sla / serviceCredits
5% of monthly fees for each 0.5 percentage point availability shortfall, capped at 15%.

5. support / supportHours
Business hours 08:00–18:00 weekdays; critical incidents have an on-call telephone channel.

6. support / supportChannels
Support channels include telephone, email and portal.

7. support / supportCommitments
An escalation manager is assigned to critical incidents.
