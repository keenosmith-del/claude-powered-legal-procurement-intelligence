Supplier B — Commercial agreement
Demonstration document. No actual supplier offer.

1. pricing / annualPrice
Annual recurring price: ZAR 2100000. VAT is excluded.

2. pricing / currency
All quoted amounts are in South African rand (ZAR).

3. pricing / setupFees
One-time implementation fee: ZAR 220000.

4. pricing / additionalCharges
Out-of-scope work requires a separately approved quotation.

5. payment / paymentDays
Invoices are payable within 60 days of receipt.

6. payment / invoicing
Recurring services are invoiced monthly in arrears.

7. payment / deposit
No deposit is required.

8. contract / durationMonths
The initial term is 36 months.

9. contract / cancellationDays
Termination permitted upon thirty days prior written notification. During the initial twelve months, 50% of the remaining annual fees become payable.

10. contract / autoRenewal
Automatically renews for twelve months unless notice is given 60 days before expiry.

11. contract / renewal
Automatically renews for twelve months unless notice is given 60 days before expiry.

12. escalation / escalationPercent
Recurring fees increase by 8% on each anniversary; the first adjustment occurs at the start of year two.

13. escalation / escalationBasis
Escalation is a fixed 8% annual percentage, not CPI-linked.

14. escalation / escalationCap
The annual escalation is capped at 8%.

15. warranty / warrantyMonths
Workmanship and replacement parts warranty lasts 24 months.

16. warranty / warrantyCoverage
Warranty covers workmanship and replacement parts.

17. warranty / warrantyExclusions
Third-party components, power faults and consequential loss are excluded.

18. liability / liabilityCapMonths
Aggregate liability is capped at 6 months of recurring fees.

19. liability / liabilityExclusions
The cap applies to all claims including data loss; fraud is excluded. No data security indemnity is offered.

20. liability / indemnities
No express IP infringement indemnity is provided.

21. penalties / penalties
No additional monetary penalties apply beyond the specified service credits.

22. penalties / breachConsequences
Material breach is subject to a 30-day cure period before termination for cause.
